# External materials for IL-24 and 6DF3

This directory retains only materials whose redistribution status is sufficiently clear for the compact release. The two supplied paper PDFs were removed because redistribution permission was not verified; they remain obtainable from their publishers or bibliographic databases and are not required for reproduction.

- `6DF3.pdb` and `6DF3.cif`: RCSB PDB structure 6DF3, available at `https://www.rcsb.org/structure/6DF3`.
- `6DF3.fasta`: RCSB entity sequences for 6DF3.
- `ELISA_competition_data.csv`: project-supplied experimental consistency data with unchanged numeric values.
- `SHA256SUMS`: integrity checks for the retained external files and this README.

In 6DF3, chain C is IL-24, chain L is IL-22R1 and chain H is IL-20R2. The structure can support posterior comparison with the IL-24–IL-22R1–IL-20R2 interface but cannot establish an IL-20R1–IL-20R2 complex or prove the antibody's competition mechanism. ELISA inhibition is posterior consistency evidence, not experimental KD or a confirmed receptor-competition IC50.

Literature associated with 6DF3 should be cited from the RCSB entry and publisher record. No paper PDF is redistributed by this package.
